# Scan Modes

OIBus uses **scan modes** to schedule
when [South connectors](../../guide/south-connectors/common-settings.md) retrieve or
when [North connectors](../../guide/north-connectors/common-settings.md) send data. Scan modes allow you to automate
data operations at specific dates and intervals.

A scan mode is either **cron-based** or **interval-based**, and can optionally be restricted to an
**activation window**.

## Default Scan Modes

OIBus provides six built-in scan modes for common scheduling needs:

| Scan Mode        | Cron Expression  | Description                                     |
| ---------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Every second     | `* * * * * *`    | Runs every second.                              |
| Every 10 seconds | `*/10 * * * * *` | Runs every 10 seconds.                          |
| Every minute     | `0 * * * * *`    | Runs every minute at the 0-second mark.         |
| Every 10 minutes | `0 */10 * * * *` | Runs every 10 minutes at the 0-second mark.     |
| Every hour       | `0 0 * * * *`    | Runs every hour at the 0-minute, 0-second mark. |
| Every 24 hours   | `0 0 0 * * *`    | Runs daily at midnight (00:00:00).              |

A seventh, non-editable **Subscription** scan mode also exists (with no cron expression) for South connectors
that push data as it arrives rather than on a schedule (e.g. MQTT, OPC UA DA). It is not displayed in the scan
mode list since it cannot be triggered or edited like the others.

## Custom Scan Modes

You can create **custom scan modes** to fit your specific scheduling requirements. Each one has a
**type**, either `Cron` or `Interval`.

## Interval Scan Modes

For the common "just poll every N seconds" case, an interval is simpler than a cron expression. Pick a
positive whole number and a unit — milliseconds, seconds, minutes or hours.

- The shortest accepted interval is **10 ms**.
- Below 1 second, OIBus shows a warning: such intervals may not be achievable in practice, depending on
  the data source and on network and system capacity. It is a warning only and does not prevent saving.

Unlike a cron expression, which is re-anchored to the wall clock on every run, an interval simply waits
the configured delay between runs and may drift slightly under heavy load.

## Activation Window

Independently of its type, a scan mode can be restricted to an **activation window**. When a scheduled
run falls outside the window it is skipped silently: nothing is queued and nothing is deferred to when
the window reopens, and no error is raised.

A window combines two optional criteria, both of which must be satisfied:

- **Date range** — an absolute start and/or end date and time. Each side is independently optional, so
  you can express "from now on", "until the end of the month", or a closed range. These are absolute
  instants, stored in UTC and displayed in your account's timezone.
- **Recurring rule** — an optional day-of-week filter (no day selected means every day) and an optional
  time-of-day range (unset means all day).

Because a recurring rule is expressed in civil time rather than as a fixed offset — "Thursday 12:00"
shifts by an hour across a daylight-saving change — it is saved together with the timezone taken from
your account settings, and re-evaluated in that timezone on every run.

### Overnight Windows

If the end time is earlier than the start time, the window spans into the next day; the form marks this
with a **+1** next to the end time. The day-of-week filter then applies to the day the window *opened*:
"Friday, 22:00–02:00" stays active from Friday 22:00 through Saturday 02:00, even though Saturday is not
itself selected.

### Examples

- *Only collect during working hours*: no date range, days Monday to Friday, time of day 08:00–18:00.
- *Only query on weekends for the next month*: date range from today to today + 1 month, days Saturday
  and Sunday, no time-of-day restriction.

Manually testing an item from the South connector page **ignores** the activation window, so a test run
is never a silent no-op.

If a window can never trigger again — for instance its end date is already past — OIBus displays a
warning on the scan mode list and on every item using that scan mode.

### Tips for Cron Expressions

- **Format**: Cron expressions consist of six fields:
  `[seconds] [minutes] [hours] [day of month] [month] [day of week]`. A 7th field (year, as used by Quartz cron)
  is not supported and will be rejected.
- **Wildcards**: Use `*` to match any value (e.g., `* * * * * *` for every second).
- **Intervals**: Use `*/` to specify intervals (e.g., `*/10` for every 10 seconds).
- **Precision**: For predictable behavior, specify exact seconds or minutes (e.g., `0` for the top of the minute/hour).
- **Non-standard characters**: `L`, `W`, `#`, `?`, and `H` are not supported and will be rejected.

#### Example Cron Expressions

- `30 * * * * *`: Runs at the 30-second mark of every minute.
- `0 15 10 * * *`: Runs daily at 10:15:00 AM.
- `0 0 22 * * 1`: Runs every Monday at 10:00:00 PM.
